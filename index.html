Professional Bio

With over 22 years of clinical and academic practice, Dr. Comfort is a leading physiotherapist specializing in manual and manipulative therapy. She holds a BMR (PT), a Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT), and a Master's in Human Physiology, and is currently pursuing a PhD in Physiotherapy. Her career is dedicated to the highest standards of patient care and the advancement of clinical education.
